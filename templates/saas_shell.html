About Fake Job AI

Enterprise‑grade rule‑based detection for fraudulent job postings.

Why it exists

Fake Job AI helps organizations protect candidates from scam postings by enforcing risk policies and highlighting anomalous patterns before jobs go live.

How detection works

A weighted rules engine evaluates email domains, compensation ranges, language cues, and missing fields to produce an interpretable risk score.

Deployment ready

Designed as an internal SaaS dashboard that can be integrated with your ATS or career site pipeline.
